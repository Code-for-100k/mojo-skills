# Claude App: Mac vs iPhone — Feature Comparison

_Last updated: June 2026_

A side-by-side look at what the **Claude desktop app for Mac** can do versus the
**Claude app for iPhone (iOS)**. The two apps share the same models and chat
experience, but each platform has a distinct set of exclusive capabilities: the
Mac is the power-user / agentic workstation, while the iPhone is the
on-the-go, sensor-and-voice client.

## TL;DR

- **Mac** is the only place with the full agentic stack: local **MCP
  connectors**, **Computer Use** (Claude drives your screen), **Cowork**
  background execution, and the **Code** tab.
- **iPhone** owns the on-device features: **voice mode**, **camera**, **Siri
  Shortcuts / widgets**, and deep **iOS integrations** (Messages, Calendar,
  Reminders, etc.).
- **Dispatch** is the bridge: kick off a task on your phone, and it runs/finishes
  on your Mac.

## Mac-only highlights

- **Local MCP connectors.** Claude Desktop is the only GUI app that supports the
  Model Context Protocol for connecting to local tools, files, and databases.
  **Desktop extensions** make installing/managing local MCP servers a single
  click (e.g., a filesystem MCP so Claude can read/write folders on your Mac, or
  a Google Drive MCP to read your Drive directly). Add them via the "+" button →
  **Connectors**.
- **Computer Use (released March 24, 2026).** On Pro and Max plans, Claude can
  see your screen, move the cursor, click, type, and interact with any app — and
  it can be driven remotely from your iPhone via Dispatch.
- **Cowork.** A background execution environment where Claude does the kind of
  work you'd do manually and keeps going while you're away.
- **Three-tab layout.** The desktop app is organized into **Chat**
  (conversations), **Cowork** (Dispatch + longer agentic work), and **Code**
  (software development — parallel sessions with git isolation, integrated
  terminal/file editor, side chats, visual diff review, app previews).

## iPhone-only highlights

- **Voice mode.** Speak to Claude and hear replies in one of five voices —
  Buttery, Airy, Mellow, Glassy, Rounded — with seamless switching between voice
  and text in the same conversation. Launched Pro-only (May 2025), now **free for
  all** (early 2026).
- **Camera.** Tap the camera button to take a photo and have Claude analyze it.
- **Siri Shortcuts, App Intents & widgets.** Launch Claude actions by voice; the
  Home Screen / Today widget has quick buttons for new chat, instant dictation,
  and camera capture.
- **Native iOS integrations.** Messages and Email (no permission required);
  Calendar, Location/Maps, Reminders, and Health (permission required) so Claude
  can read context from and act with your phone's data.

## Shared features

Both apps run the same Claude models, support **Projects** you've already created,
display **Artifacts inside conversations**, and let you attach files (drag-and-drop
on Mac, the share sheet on iOS). Conversation history syncs across devices through
your account.

> Note: Mobile apps can't **create** new Projects or browse the dedicated
> **Artifacts library** — those need the web or desktop app for initial setup.
> You can still work inside existing Projects and view Artifacts in active chats.

## The bridge: Dispatch

**Dispatch** ties the two together. Assign Claude a task from your iPhone and come
back to finished work on your Mac — including remotely triggering **Computer Use**
so Claude operates your Mac while you're away from it.

## Which should you use?

- **Reach for the Mac** for coding, multi-step agentic work, anything touching
  local files/databases/tools, screen automation, or long background jobs.
- **Reach for the iPhone** for hands-free voice, snapping photos, quick captures
  via widget/Siri, and tasks that draw on your calendar, reminders, messages, or
  location — or to kick off work that finishes on your Mac.
